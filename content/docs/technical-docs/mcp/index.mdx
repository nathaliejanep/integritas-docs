## Overview

The **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** lets you use Integritas directly from AI assistants Claude and ChatGPT. Once connected, you can stamp check and verify data, without leaving your workflow.

---

## Connect your assistant

Choose your assistant and follow the setup steps:

- **[Connecting with Claude →](/docs/technical-docs/mcp/connecting-claude)**
- **[Connecting with GPT →](/docs/technical-docs/mcp/connecting-gpt)**

After configuration, you can use Integritas tools (e.g. stamp hashes, verify data, get proofs) from your AI assistant.
